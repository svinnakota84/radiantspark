RADIANT SPARK GLOBAL

Customer & privacy information

This website introduces owner-described business services, business divisions, a product finder and an owner agent workspace. It does not certify entity registrations, operating licenses, product availability or service capacity.

Business inquiries

A direct engagement needs a verified contact channel and an agreed written scope, price, delivery milestones, payment terms, support responsibilities and cancellation or refund terms. The website does not itself accept product orders, reserve stock, send shipments, make payments or file documents. Product and division images illustrate concepts.

Retailer discovery

Retailer links open separate services. Confirm the seller, current price, taxes, delivery, returns and warranty there before purchasing. Curated search links have no displayed price. If the owner configures a product feed, prices are supplied by that feed and shown with its timestamp; this site does not independently verify them. A stale, invalid or future timestamp suppresses a price. Approved Amazon affiliate tags enable an Associate disclosure; configured external feeds may contain their own referral parameters.

Ten software assistant roles

The agent workspace is for the owner, not public customer account access. It requires a deployed backend, server credentials, approved toolkits and connected app accounts. A configured backend is not proof of a working connection; a successful run verifies only that individual request. Roles are software assistants, not human employees, and no performance statistics are invented.

The current backend supports read-only research and draft responses. It blocks create, update and destructive tool categories, remote code execution, proxy execution and connection management. It cannot send messages, purchase, pay, publish, create invoices or change production records. Generated answers can be incomplete or wrong and need owner review; finance and policy roles do not replace qualified professionals.

Data and privacy

The public website does not collect card numbers, identity documents or health profiles. Configured contact links open your email client. Hosting, external fonts and image providers may process connection information under their policies. No advertising analytics is added by this build.

The agent owner access token stays in tab memory; it is not written to browser storage. When an authorized owner submits a connected task, the task text and relevant connected records are processed by the configured backend, model provider, Composio and approved app services. Only submit data you are authorized to process. Provider retention policies apply; this website does not promise zero retention. The handler does not write prompts or records into application error logs; it records a request ID and role for failed runs.

Lifestyle Hub is a separate site. Its saved recipe IDs stay in the visitor's browser, as explained in its own privacy information. There is no cross-site member account or shared health profile.

Payments and legal rights

Any available Lifestyle payment link opens a Stripe-hosted page. Review the actual service description and terms there. Payment does not activate a member account on this static website. Personalized coaching, paid fulfillment and daily messaging require their own verified backend integrations.

Applicable consumer rights remain in force. No immunity, blanket waiver, legal certification, health outcome or financial result is promised. Obtain qualified advice for the actual business activity and applicable requirements.
